Baudsmith Studios is the home for a collection of projects born from real problems — the kind you run into when you're deep in a misconfigured homelab at 2am. The work here spans networking, Raspberry Pi, HAM radio, and the connective tissue between them.

These projects are lean, focused, and opinionated — built around specific workflows, specific hardware, and a particular way of thinking about problems. They won't be for everyone, but when they fit, they fit well.

If something here can work with your setup, try it out. If it almost works, fork it and make it yours.
