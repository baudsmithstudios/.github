Baudsmith Studios is the home for a collection of projects that span networking, HAM radio, and small-scale homelabbing using raspberry pi kits.

These projects are lean, focused, and opinionated. They are built around specific workflows, specific hardware, and a particular way of thinking about problems. They won't be for everyone, but when they fit, they fit well.

If something here can work with your setup, try it out. If it almost works, fork it and make it yours.
